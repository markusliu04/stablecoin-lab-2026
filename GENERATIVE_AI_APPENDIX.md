# Generative AI Appendix

## Human contributions

The student proposed the solution approach and supplied substantive responses to the
assignment questions. The student obtained and managed the test-wallet private key and
API credentials, requested Sepolia test ETH from a faucet, and carried out or authorized
the compilation, command-line operations, deployment and verification workflow.

## Generative AI contributions

Codex/ChatGPT and Gemini assisted with drafting and revising the Solidity code and tests,
developing explanatory drafts, and polishing the answers and report. The tools also helped
interpret compiler, test and deployment output and organize the submission evidence.
Gemini assisted with reading back and reviewing the reported results.

## Review and responsibility

The student is responsible for the submitted work, the technical decisions and the final
review of generated material. Test logs, contract addresses and verification links are
provided as evidence rather than treating an AI tool's statement as proof of completion.
This appendix records the division of work; it does not claim that the lab handout itself
requires an AI declaration.

## Credential handling

Private keys, API credentials and `.env` files are excluded from the submission. Only public
contract addresses, transaction hashes and publicly known local Anvil fixture keys appear
in the teaching materials. No private credential values are included in this appendix.
